Hamilton Thor 22 Jewel Wrist Watch (Grade 770)

Hamilton Thor Rectangular Gold Tone Case (S492054)

Overview

The Hamilton Thor is a late 1950s to early 1960s rectangular dress watch powered by the Grade 770, Hamiltons final high grade American movement. With 22 jewels, shock protection, and elegant styling, the Thor represents the end of Hamilton’s Lancaster, Pennsylvania production era.

Model Hamilton Thor (Thor B / Thor CL variant)
Movement Hamilton Grade 770, manual wind
Jewels 22 jewels
Dial Silver, gold markers, triangle markers at 3 9 12, sub seconds at 6
Hands Gold tone
Case Rectangular gold tone
Case Serial S492054 (Star Watch Case Co.)
Production Era c. 1958 to 1962
Origin Lancaster, Pennsylvania, USA

Historical Significance

The Thor showcases Hamiltons finest American craftsmanship. The Grade 770 movement is widely regarded as one of the best American wristwatch movements ever produced, and the Thors distinctive triangular markers and sub seconds dial make it one of Hamiltons most elegant mid century designs.
