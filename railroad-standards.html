Railroad Watch Standards

The Requirements of a True Railroad Watch

During the golden era of American railroading, accurate timekeeping was essential for safety. Railroad companies enforced strict standards for watches used by conductors, engineers, and brakemen.

  • Open-face case
  • Size 16 or 18
  • Minimum 17 jewels
  • Adjusted to at least 5 positions
  • Adjusted to temperature
  • Adjusted to isochronism
  • Lever-set
  • Bold Arabic numerals
  • Plain white dial
  • Steel escape wheel (later years)
  • Daily inspection by a time inspector
